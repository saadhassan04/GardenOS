# GardenOS — Product Vision

## Mission
Be the complete, lifelong digital operating system for a home garden — capturing, organizing, and eventually understanding every plant, every care action, every harvest, and every photograph across decades — while remaining free, private, and fully usable offline.

## Why GardenOS Exists
Real gardens hold years of knowledge that normally lives in memory, scattered photos, and guesswork: when the plumeria last flowered, what cured the mealybugs, how much the tomatoes actually yielded, which balcony corner scorches in June. Generic apps are online-only, ad-supported, subscription-gated, or abandoned. GardenOS exists so a gardener owns that knowledge permanently, on their own device, in open formats, for as long as they garden.

## Problems GardenOS Solves
- **Forgotten care history** → an event-sourced timeline per plant (watering, feeding, pruning, pests, treatments, harvests, photos, milestones).
- **Guesswork care** → Karachi-tuned, seasonal care profiles that drive a "needs attention" view; recurring tasks with a real recurrence engine.
- **Lost visual progress** → per-plant growth photo galleries.
- **"What worked last time?"** → pest/treatment history with outcomes.
- **Yield blindness** → sowing lifecycle + per-crop/season harvest totals.
- **Vendor risk & cost** → zero dependencies, zero cost, no accounts, no servers; robust local backup/restore.
- **Connectivity gaps** → 100% offline function; network only ever enhances.

## Core Principles
1. Offline-first, forever.
2. The user owns their data (on-device, open formats, exportable).
3. Free and open — no paid services, no lock-in.
4. Longevity over fashion — standards, not frameworks; built to run in 2046.
5. Privacy by default — nothing leaves the device without explicit opt-in.
6. Climate-aware — anchored to the gardener's real environment (Karachi).
7. AI that serves and explains — suggests, never overrides; grounded in the garden's own data.
8. Enterprise-quality engineering at any scale — tested, documented, no placeholders.

## Future Vision
GardenOS grows in eras without breaking its promises: from the offline core (v1.x, complete) into plant/disease vision (v2.0), weather intelligence (v2.5), a grounded Garden AI of doctors and advisors (v3.0), optional end-to-end-encrypted multi-device sync (v4.0), IoT sensor integration (v5.0), and finally a spatial garden digital twin that can simulate and plan (v6.0). Each era plugs into interfaces and reserved data stores that already exist today.

## 20-Year Vision
By 2046, GardenOS holds the complete life story of a garden: thousands of photographs organized into growth timelines, decades of harvest analytics, a searchable history of every outbreak and its cure, on-device AI that recognizes each individual plant and its condition, sensors reporting soil moisture in real time, and a digital twin that answers real planning questions better than intuition. The data is still the user's, still local-first, still readable — because every decision from day one was made for that horizon.

## Future Ecosystem Integration (exploratory, never at the cost of the core)
- Local-network IoT (Web Bluetooth / Web Serial / local HTTP) for sensors and, later, actuators.
- Optional user-owned/free-tier encrypted sync and off-device backup targets.
- Import/export bridges to open horticultural datasets and the local knowledge graph.
- Shareable read-only garden snapshots (static export) — no account required.
- Voice interaction via on-device Web Speech.

Every integration is opt-in, privacy-preserving, and must leave GardenOS fully functional when absent.
